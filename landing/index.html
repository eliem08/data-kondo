Who it's for

Ops and data teams preparing spreadsheets and exports for AI projects.

The problem

Agents choke on inconsistent CSVs: mixed dates, duplicate rows, junk columns and broken encodings.

Quickstart

Python 3.10+, no other dependencies. Your release archive includes the wheel and examples.

pip install data_kondo-0.1.0-py3-none-any.whl
data-kondo profile examples/customers.csv
data-kondo clean examples/customers.csv clean.csv --key e_mail
